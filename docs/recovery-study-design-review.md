# Prospective closed-loop recovery study: independent design review

Status: design recommendations, 19 October 2026. This document is prospective and is not an execution receipt, a frozen protocol, or a result. It reviews the current manuscript, review response, README, application integration and stable API documentation, and the public retry-advice boundary. The reviewer did not inspect the new held-out case file or any future measured results and did not run the application, controllers, models, or APIs. The final machine-readable protocol must adopt or explicitly reject these recommendations before collection.

## Research contribution and scope

The useful next experiment is a matched **closed-loop application study**: deterministic policy state machines choose whether to wait, read, stop, retry, or escalate against an isolated SQLite application whose effects really change subsequent observations. Inventory, tickets, and artifact records extend the application boundary beyond the refund fixture. They remain AI-authored challenge tasks on one local database substrate. This is not a population sample of tasks, an autonomous LLM-agent benchmark, a production service, a causal-attribution comparison, or a demonstration of a new estimator.

A falsifiable contribution is an executable recovery protocol that makes three boundaries testable: contract satisfaction versus source-supported origin; historical absence versus the resolution of an in-flight attempt; and correctness of a final claim versus whether the available evidence supported that claim when issued. The study should test whether using those distinctions changes safe completion, harmful effects, abstention, and resource use. The equally informed independent reference is the strong methodological comparator. Exact parity with it is legitimate evidence of implementation agreement; it is not an advantage over that reference. Any advantage over response-only or state-only policies identifies the value of the chosen evidence/policy distinction in this suite, not novelty of verification, provenance, or idempotency.

The existing manuscript already acknowledges same-information parity, late commits, the absence of a measured controller, and the limits of AI-assisted checking. The new study should close a specific controller-evaluation gap without relabeling the previous factual, benchmark-transfer, or native CAR studies.

## The temporal problem must be resolved before freezing

For each case declare registration time t0, the unchanged original completion deadline D, the original stability horizon H = D + W, a separately predeclared recovery deadline R with persistence horizon H_R = R + W_R, and a later safety-audit horizon S >= max(H, H_R). Also declare the final evidence collection cutoff C. Ordering, inclusive boundary semantics, pending-attempt treatment, and the event queue drain rule must be fixed. D, H, R, S, and C are distinct concepts even if some coincide in a particular case.

A complete observation through H can establish that an original temporal contract was violated. It cannot establish that a timed-out attempt will never commit after H. A new effect after D also cannot rescue satisfaction of that original deadline. A record verifier's `unknown` before its horizon is expected temporal behavior, rather than a reason to fabricate an early negative receipt.

The current public `retry_advice` describes `RETRY_PERMITTED` as no relevant effect observed in complete readback through the horizon and explicitly warns that an in-flight attempt may still commit later. The study controller therefore needs an application-level safety gate. Calling that gate part of the experimental policy is accurate; attributing attempt finality to ConseqaX verification is not.

Choose and freeze one of the following designs, or predeclare both as separate capability strata:

1. **Provider-resolved recovery.** The provider exposes an operation/attempt status with terminal semantics: an accepted terminal-no-effect result guarantees that attempt cannot subsequently commit. A cancellation receipt qualifies only if it means cancellation completed with that guarantee, rather than cancellation requested. Alternatively, the provider enforces atomic deduplication for the stable operation key and payload over the entire relevant interval. Every controller receives the same public capability declaration and the same available status/cancellation receipts. Productive retries before D can then be evaluated when enough time remains. These guarantees are properties of the simulated provider, not properties established by the framework.
2. **Unresolved requests and conservative abstention.** Without deduplication, a finite in-flight bound, or terminal resolution, evidence-aware policies wait or escalate while an earlier request might commit. They may miss an otherwise recoverable deadline. This is a safety/availability tradeoff, and the missed completion must count. A response-driven or bounded-absence retry heuristic can remain as a declared comparison policy; any delayed duplicate is scored through S. Absence of a duplicate in this finite experiment is not an exactly-once guarantee.

A known finite bound B is usable only if it covers every outstanding attempt, uses the declared clock, and is an enforceable provider property. Waiting until B and observing a complete snapshot is then a conditional resolution strategy. Do not create B by looking at the last event in evaluator truth. In an unbounded-late-commit stratum, S is a measurement cutoff, not proof that no future effect is possible. Record outstanding requests at S and report them separately.

An early terminal-no-effect receipt may justify another dispatch while the original outcome receipt remains unknown. Keep both records with their separate meanings. A later recovery objective must be declared at t0 or by a fixed public rule before the outcome is seen; it cannot overwrite D or replace an original failure with success. Original satisfaction and later recovery require separate scores.

Terminal resolution of the agent's attempt also does not prevent a different actor's future write. If global exactly-one safety requires a scope-level unique constraint, lock, or shared provider operation identity, that is another explicit provider guarantee. Cases with concurrent external actors should expose this limit rather than silently assuming exclusive access.

## Controller and idempotency fairness

Freeze complete transition tables for five policies: `response_retry` (response-driven same-key retry), a readback-state ablation, an independent same-information provenance reference, `consequax_one_read`, and `consequax_poll`. The last two use the public ConseqaX API with the separately declared study application safety gate. A provider response field `terminal_not_committed` may be used only when the provider contract guarantees that the terminated attempt can never commit later; every arm receives that same public field when it exists. A missing field supplies no terminal guarantee. Each table must specify actions for every response type, unknown/violated/satisfied state, external/unknown origin, pending attempt, budget boundary, deadline boundary, and malformed/unavailable readback. An unhandled transition is a retained protocol failure, not an opportunity to add a special case after collection.

The independent reference must not call ConseqaX, import its private verifier logic, or receive evaluator truth. It should implement the declared contract and provenance rules independently from the public specification. Shared generic serialization or provider interfaces are acceptable and must be disclosed. Avoid sharing the semantic linkage or target-classification helper between reference and CX; a shared helper can make both arms reproduce the same defect. CX should consume public receipt fields, while the reference derives its conclusion independently. Distinct implementations of one declared policy test conformance, not competing policy ideas.

All policies must use the same stable logical operation identifier, the same provider-scoped idempotency key when supported, and the same payload binding. Retries change attempt identity while preserving logical identity. Freeze key namespace, retention duration, behavior while the first attempt is pending, concurrent-key handling, and conflicting-payload rejection. Giving reliable deduplication only to ConseqaX would invalidate the comparison.

A strong classical baseline is: preserve the operation key and payload; retry only under the published provider semantics; query authoritative operation status or readback; stop when fulfilled; retain unresolved requests. If the independent reference supplies this behavior, identify it explicitly as the classical reference. The `response_retry` arm is also a classical same-key retry baseline in provider-idempotent strata. If the reference and response-retry arms together still omit an applicable classical recovery feature, add that feature before freezing or narrow the comparison claim. Report the incremental effect of provenance separately from the benefit already obtained by ordinary idempotent retries and authoritative reads. If state-only and provenance-aware arms use identical action transition rules, explicitly state that provenance is an explanation ablation and cannot establish an action/recovery advantage in this design. If generic `retry_advice` is recorded but not used to choose actions, describe the measured policy as receipt-based application logic rather than a measured benefit of that advice interface. A non-idempotent fault stratum is informative only when all arms face that same provider limitation.

State-only and response-only are information-use ablations. Offer the same public observations and provider-capability records at the same decision times, and specify which fields each ablation ignores. Do not call an advantage over these policies superiority over an equally informed strong method.

## Paired worlds, visibility, and sources

Each scheduled case/controller cell starts from a fresh database and an identical frozen exogenous event schedule. Controller actions create branch-specific effects; after actions diverge, readback bytes may legitimately diverge. Pair the environment, provider law, read opportunity, source lag, and budget rules rather than replaying one controller's already realized observation bytes into another world. Use deterministic event-order tie breaking, including events exactly at D, H, R, or S.

The public observation allowlist should contain request and contract, action/attempt identities, dispatch and response history, provider capability/status receipts, and readback with source, declared scope, completeness, observation time, watermark, record history, and available provenance. Exclude case/family/fault/split labels, generator seeds or filenames that encode them, queued future events, evaluator-only truth, and any other controller's decisions or receipts. Neutral identifiers should preserve equality and joins without revealing the mechanism.

An independent read-only connection is a path-separation property within this simulation; it is not an independently operated source. Declare why the application's SQLite source is authoritative, what history it exposes, and how watermark, completeness, and record timestamps are obtained. A current row alone does not prove persistence throughout a past interval. Absence requires exhaustive scope through the relevant cutoff; positive presence and negative absence may require different evidence. Wrong scope, unavailable reads, insufficient history, missing fields, or stale watermarks remain unknown. Missing provenance does not become other-actor origin, and a trustworthy state witness does not automatically make its actor field trustworthy.

Freeze source-trust and clock-scope assumptions for every stratum. Provider status and database readback may be different sources with different guarantees. Distinguish simulated logical time, host elapsed time, and any database time. Never compare timestamps across clocks without a declared conversion/bound. Hashes establish which bytes were used, not whether the provider or source told the truth.

## Held-out task design and independence

Use at least twelve new task families and at least two cases per family, spanning the declared application domains. A family is a shared task mechanism/template with related cases; changing identifiers, wording, or seeds does not create another independent family. Include clean controls and faults that are not recoverable within the allotted budget. Include missing provenance, external actors, wrong scope, lag, false success, partial/wrong effects, removals or reversals, and delayed commits whose timing challenges a bounded-absence retry.

The final case inventory should cross capability strata with fault mechanisms sufficiently to avoid making non-idempotency synonymous with a particular policy-favorable fault. Do not force every family into every cell merely to inflate counts. Freeze a coverage matrix and disclose gaps.

Controller authors may use a separate development corpus and public semantics. The new family definitions, fault compositions, and case bytes must remain unseen by controller authors until their policies and analysis are frozen. AI authorship by a separate agent is procedural separation within an AI-assisted workflow, not independent human authorship or a random population sample. If the author or lead integrator inspects the corpus before the joint freeze, disclose that exposure and describe it as prospectively collected on a new frozen challenge set rather than fully blinded external validation.

Include observationally indistinguishable prefixes with different later hidden outcomes. No policy can infer which hidden future will occur from identical admissible history. Such cases test appropriate waiting or abstention and make impossible guarantees visible. Their hidden labels must not leak through task text, IDs, evidence shape, or the read schedule.

## Recommended predeclared endpoints

For controller m, family f, and every scheduled case c define the following from a separately implemented evaluator over the full event ledger. Freeze field-level and temporal predicates, including exact-count semantics, before collection.

| Endpoint | Definition and denominator |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary: safe recovery Y** | One if the predeclared recovery contract meets its payload/count target by R and persists through H_R, with no prespecified harmful/unwanted effect through S. Otherwise zero. Every scheduled cell is in the denominator; execution failure, budget exhaustion, or unstarted execution cannot earn success. An effect after D can meet recovery while the original SLA remains violated. |
| **Primary safety companion HARM** | One if an extra, wrong-scope, wrong-payload, forbidden, or otherwise prespecified unwanted effect occurs through S, even if subsequently compensated. Keep all cells in the denominator and show unknown audit outcomes separately. Report harm counts and severity categories without hiding them inside Y. |
| Original contract satisfaction | Exact original D/H predicate, independent of later recovery. Global state satisfied by another actor can meet this target. It does not establish agent-produced success. |
| Supported linked completion | Completion plus trustworthy qualifying linkage to this operation. Missing origin remains unknown; an externally satisfied task is a separate state success. Report controller-created effects and dispatch exposure so external/no-action successes cannot be described as successful execution. |
| Claim validity and support | State, origin, and safe-to-retry assertions separately: truth-correct, truth-wrong, truth-unassessable; and evidence-supported, unsupported, or abstained at issuance. An unsupported correct guess is not supported success. |
| Coverage and abstention | Definite claims / all scheduled or assessable cells; errors / definite assessable claims; correct supported yield / all scheduled cells. Report both denominators. Zero definite claims gives undefined conditional error, not zero error. |
| Unresolved work | Pending attempts at D, R, and S; escalations; reads that remain insufficient; and missed recovery opportunities under the fixed policy and budget. Do not let a policy win solely by never acting. |
| Cost | Actual and refused dispatch/read/status/cancellation requests, simulation time to completion/decision, host elapsed time, and trace/receipt bytes. Report every arm's exact spend and caps. |

Make Y the single efficacy endpoint and HARM its mandatory safety companion. The recommended primary contrast is the equal-family paired difference in Y between `consequax_poll` and `response_retry`. Predeclare poll minus state-only as secondary, poll minus one-read as the polling ablation, and poll/reference agreement as the strong implementation check (or a declared policy comparison if their transitions differ). Do not choose whichever contrast looks strongest afterward. A positive efficacy difference alone never warrants a safety-improvement claim; inspect paired HARM and unresolved requests directly.

For each family let y_fm be the mean of Y across **all scheduled cases** in that family, including any fixed repetitions, and d_f = y_f,CX - y_f,comparison. Report Delta = mean_f d_f, the full table of family-level pairs/differences, and exact finite-suite counts. Equal family weight prevents large template families from dominating. Repetitions refine a fixed family's result; they do not increase the inferential family count. Report unweighted cell totals as a secondary operational view.

The evaluator must retain original task state, linked state, harmful effects, and supported claims as separate outputs. A known global contract violation makes action-linked contract satisfaction false, even when an own matching witness exists. Keep mere own-witness presence as a separately named variable; it cannot rescue a failed exact-count contract. A failed runner cannot silently receive an invented violation label. For Y's all-slot operational score it earns no success; semantic outputs remain missing/unknown, with an explicit completion-status partition. If the safety audit itself fails, provide lower/upper harm bounds by treating unknown audit cells first as no harm and then as harm. Never call the observed lower bound the actual safety rate.

## Precision, inference, and multiplicity plan

Twelve purposively AI-authored families support a small challenge-suite study. The exact suite difference has no sampling error conditional on these deterministic cases. It does not acquire a task-population confidence interval because there are many controller cells or repeated seeds.

Use equal-family finite-suite estimates and all paired family differences as the primary analysis. Do not calculate a population p-value or task-population confidence interval for this purposive corpus. Similar authorship, domains, and mechanisms make a hypothetical independent-family model especially doubtful. If stochastic simulation seeds are later sampled prospectively from a declared seed distribution, conditional seed Monte Carlo uncertainty can be reported for the fixed suite with family structure preserved; it describes that seed law and supplies no uncertainty statement about new task families. Deterministic repeated executions create no Monte Carlo sample at all.

A bounded-distribution planning calculation emphasizes the limitation. Under independent sampled families, a 95% Hoeffding half-width for paired d_f in [-1,1] is sqrt(2 log(40) / F), approximately 0.784 at F=12. Achieving half-width 0.10 by that worst-case criterion requires 738 families, not 738 episodes. A variance-based planning approximation is F >= (1.96 s_d / h)^2, followed by small-sample inflation and an externally justified pilot variance. Do not estimate s_d on the held-out measured result and then claim the sample size was planned from it.

Zero observed harms is also weak evidence at this scale. Under independent sampled binary family-level any-harm outcomes, 0/12 would still have a one-sided 95% upper bound of about 22.1%. This illustration is not a valid bound for a purposively authored suite without that sampling assumption. Within-case replication cannot replace independently sampled families.

Freeze the exact primary contrast and report its finite-suite value without a task-population interval. Any conditional seed-law interval must name its sampling mechanism and denominator; multiple such intervals are descriptive unless a simultaneous procedure and its family of comparisons are specified prospectively. In particular, endpoint, family, domain, fault, and budget subgroups are not independent replications. If more families are required for a population claim, add them before collection with the same concealment rules; do not extend or stop after seeing the primary effect. Avoid an equivalence/noninferiority claim against the reference unless a substantive margin and adequate family-level precision were established before collection. Exact observed parity needs no such inferential embellishment.

## Matched budget and trajectory accounting

Freeze per-cell dispatch cap, read cap, status/cancellation cap, read opportunities, action deadlines, simulation wait budget, and final audit budget. If status/cancellation calls are implemented as reads, specify their charge explicitly. Equal maximum budgets do not imply equal spend; unused budget is a policy outcome. Record requested, admitted, completed, failed, refused, and unstarted operations separately. Count unsuccessful requests and timeout dispatches, not only committed effects.

At each decision opportunity record public inputs, decision, remaining budgets, logical clock, and the reason for refusing any operation. If all arms receive compulsory readbacks, charge those reads identically even when a policy ignores them. If reads are optional, offer identical opportunities and charge actual requests; report access parity and cost differences. Do not give the CX controller free verifier reads, extra retries, privileged preflights, or source-status calls. Evaluator audit reads do not go to controllers and appear as a separate cost category.

Primary cost reporting should be a per-family paired table of dispatches, independent reads, provider-resolution calls, and logical time, followed by total spend. Provide safe completions per dispatch/read only with its denominator; a zero-success cost-per-success is undefined. Keep simulated latency separate from machine runtime. One local run cannot establish intrinsic runtime overhead or production charges.

## Freeze and first-run integrity

Before measured execution publish or otherwise preserve a signed-off protocol and SHA-256 inventory binding: all corpus bytes and split assignment; family/case schedule; provider semantics and event-order rules; source authority/clock rules; controller source and transition tables; installed package/version artifact and dependencies; evaluator and analysis source; evidence allowlist; budgets; endpoint definitions; precision/multiplicity rules; and stopping/failure rules. Record environment and hardware. A commit alone is insufficient if uncommitted files determine execution; bind their exact bytes too. Hashes are integrity evidence, not authentication of a source's claims.

The complete planned Cartesian schedule must exist before the first measured cell. Retain every cell, including malformed records, unknowns, failures, refused requests, audit failures, budget exhaustion, and unstarted cells. A semantic mismatch with the strong reference triggers a visible investigation; it is not a reason to omit a case. Specify whether a critical defect stops remaining collection. Preserve all first-run outputs, the failure record, and the unchanged original denominator.

Do not repair the controller or case and silently reuse the original study label. A repaired run is a new version with a new freeze and explicit exposed-data status. Deterministic re-execution can later check reproducibility; it cannot replace the first measured run or create extra independent trials. No best seed, best rerun, or post-result case removal is permitted. Genuine malformed cases receive a retained invalid-case category and all-slot operational accounting; any sensitivity analysis excluding them must keep the original analysis alongside it.

## Independent human checking: prospective packet

Human review remains pending until actual people are recruited. AI-written labels, AI audits, self-review, and blank annotation packs are not human annotation. Recruit at least two people who did not implement the policies, verifiers, or evaluator; record their expertise, conflicts, training, paid/unpaid status, and exact independent roles with consent. Resolve the sample size and packet inventory before revealing measured controller outcomes to annotators. For a small suite, double-annotate all final cell packets rather than selecting only disagreements or dramatic faults. Decision-level sampling, if necessary, must be fixed by a neutral rule before collection.

Use aliases and randomized packet order. Hide controller names, fault/family labels, study hypotheses, framework receipts/diagnosis codes, evaluator labels, and other annotators' decisions. A support packet contains the original request/contract, provider guarantees, clock/source definitions, the raw admissible history through the assertion time, and the exact assertion/action to judge. It must not contain future evidence. Ask annotators separately whether state, agent origin, and safe retry are supported, contradicted, or indeterminate from that evidence, and require cited witness IDs plus a short reason. Include explicit abstention and unsupported-correct-guess rubric examples from development cases only.

Seal independent support labels before providing any fuller evaluator ledger for a separate truth/contract audit. That later audit can inspect late commits, event timing, count/persistence, and evaluator correctness; it must not retroactively supply support for an earlier assertion. Keep support and hindsight truth labels distinct. Report initial labels, category confusion matrices, raw agreement, category prevalences, and Cohen's kappa where defined; disclose undefined/unstable kappa when categories collapse. A third independent adjudicator can resolve disagreements after initial labels are locked. Publish disagreement and adjudication counts and both pre- and post-adjudication scoring. Annotator items are clustered within trajectories and families; do not count every sentence as an independent participant or task.

If humans identify a verifier/evaluator defect, preserve the original machine score and first-run evidence, log the defect, and report a separately versioned corrected analysis. Do not label the machine gold independently validated until that checking has actually occurred. Annotation agreement concerns consistency; it alone does not prove the source or predicate is true.

## Minimal analyses and permitted claims

The minimum results package should contain the schedule/disposition partition; all family-by-controller endpoint and cost rows; the one primary paired contrast; paired harmful-effect counts and pending requests; original versus recovery deadlines; supported-claim coverage, errors and abstentions; capability/fault/domain strata as descriptive checks; all strong-reference discrepancies, including agreement on unknown; and an exact inventory of failures and protocol deviations. Include at least one complete neutralized trajectory for each mechanism, selected by a predeclared rule, with public and evaluator-only records clearly separated. No causal estimator, LLM judge, or extra benchmark run is necessary for this first controller experiment.

A defensible positive conclusion would be that the frozen policies on the new AI-authored challenge suite changed safe completion and/or unsupported claims at the reported resource cost, with an independent same-information implementation agreeing or disagreeing as observed. A null or adverse result is equally informative: classical idempotency may erase the apparent advantage, provenance may change explanations without improving actions, and conservative waiting may reduce harm while missing deadlines. No result here establishes general superiority, authenticated provenance, arbitrary future finality, autonomous benchmark reward, or independent human verification before humans complete the packet.

The next genuine external evaluation should use independently selected tasks on a maintained application/agent benchmark or provider sandbox, a real frozen agent policy, matched classical recovery controls, independently audited source/operation semantics, and the native task reward plus side-effect ledger. Choose one such environment and preregister before seeing its held-out outcomes; adding more internal scripted cases is not a substitute.
