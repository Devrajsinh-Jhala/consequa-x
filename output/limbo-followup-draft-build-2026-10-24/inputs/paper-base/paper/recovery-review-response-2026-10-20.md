# Research-review addendum — 20 October 2026

This addendum addresses the paper's research criticisms, excluding the business review. It supplements [the earlier response](review-response-2026-10-18.md); it does not declare every criticism resolved or establish suitability for a selective conference. The [focused manuscript](conseqax-research.tex) now centers a bounded question: when do admitted evidence and provider guarantees justify recovery decisions, and what completion, harm, reporting, and resource costs follow?

This document records the completed, audited objective Studies A and B and the limits of their conclusions. The [Study A findings](recovery-study-findings-2026-10-20.md), [paper results input](recovery-study-results.tex), and [controlled availability results](recovery-availability-results.tex) retain the exact outcomes. These experiments address narrower software/systems questions than general autonomous-agent performance or conference readiness.

## What changed in response to the research criticisms

| Criticism | Concrete revision and remaining interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| The contribution overlaps existing verification, provenance, idempotency, and attribution work. | The manuscript narrows the contribution to an executable evidence protocol and conditional recovery evaluation. It treats response/effect separation, idempotency, three-valued conclusions, and attribution estimators as established foundations. Its short indistinguishability argument states a known retry boundary, without a novelty-priority claim. |
| The evaluation lacked a matched closed-loop controller comparison. | Study A completed all 15,360 scheduled worlds across five policies, 24 authored cases in 12 purposive families, three nonpayment SQLite domains, and 128 seeds. All recorded metrics are invariant across seeds within each of 120 case-policy groups; repetitions do not add endpoint diversity. Policies act on fresh databases and change subsequent observations. This tests deterministic application recovery, rather than autonomous LLM behavior or production-service reliability. Separate AI authorship is procedural separation within one project, not external replication. |
| Baselines were weak or could receive unequal capabilities. | Response-driven same-key retry, state-only readback, a separate evidence implementation, package-backed one-final-read, and package-backed bounded polling share public native-idempotency capability, terminal-resolution guarantees, budgets, and read opportunities. Branch-specific actions can legitimately produce different later observations. Equal access does not mean equal actual query spend. |
| Reference agreement could be mistaken for superiority. | The reference computes evidence conclusions independently of package verification, but shares the application state machine and was written by the controller author. Agreement therefore supports observed conformance only. Because provenance-aware and state-only policies share action transitions, their contrast concerns reporting, not a provenance-caused recovery benefit. |
| An empty readback was being confused with request finality. | Without admitted native deduplication, a retry needs terminal-no-future-effect guarantees for every previous attempt, fresh complete trusted absence, remaining budget, and time before the original deadline. A watermark certifies historical coverage only. Provider guarantees and source admission are application assumptions; package advice alone does not authorize the dispatch. |
| Deadline changes and polling could manufacture success. | The original deadline **D** and stability horizon **H** remain fixed. Recovery is separately evaluated by **R**, with persistence and harm checked through finite cutoff **S**. Later recovery cannot repair an original SLA violation. The single-read arm receives one final horizon-eligible read and may make gate-relevant predeadline queries; continued polling must be assessed against its actual additional cost. |
| Missingness and repeated seeds could inflate the evidence. | The primary operational safe-recovery endpoint uses every scheduled slot. Execution failure earns zero operational success while unavailable physical and semantic outcomes remain unknown. Secondary semantic rates retain observed/unavailable denominators. Analysis reports exact finite-suite counts, paired contrasts, and all family differences; it does not turn 128 seeds into independent tasks or attach task-population confidence intervals or p-values to purposively authored families. |
| Correct final guesses were being confused with evidence support. | Full-ledger hindsight correctness, definite-report yield, and abstention are distinct from support at issuance. A correct guess does not prove support. The current paper evaluates executable contracts, effect-ledger truth, reference conformance, and automated artifact consistency; it does not claim interpretability, preference, rationale quality, or independently measured evidence support at issuance. |
| Polling needed an actual availability-versus-cost test. | Study A found no incremental polling utility under its schedules. A separately registered, post hoc motivated Study B completed the entire 64-profile availability/lag/completeness grid in three domains with five policies: 960/960 worlds. Polling improves correct-state coverage over one final read by 78/192 at additional read/time cost; the equally informed reference and state-only rule share that gain, and physical recovery is identical. The original negative result remains reported. |
| Ground truth and auditing were insufficiently separated. | Full retained SQLite/effect-ledger histories supply the objective state, linkage, harm, and temporal targets. Separately implemented auditors query retained snapshots read-only and recompute outcomes; they do not authenticate the provider or prove source semantics. During collection, readback is an application method on the same SQLite ledger/connection, separate from the ACK signal rather than an independent source or trust domain. These checks reduce arithmetic/retention uncertainty within the controlled substrate, without claiming external service validation. |
| Package maturity was being conflated with research validation. | The product is published as [2.1.2](../output/release-2.1.2-2026-10-20/README.md); Studies A and B retain pinned 2.1.1. The paper evaluates selected recording/contract/observation/record-verification paths and explicit controller code, not every release feature, the public blocking polling helper, SDK/telemetry effectiveness, or production reliability. Historical 1.1.0/2.0 evidence retains its own versions. |

## What the completed comparisons establish

In Study A, every readback policy achieved 1,792/3,072 operational safe recoveries, versus 1,024/3,072 for the declared response-driven rule. The equal-family difference is +0.25: three positive families and nine zero. One gain depends on the response baseline's explicit-error stopping rule, so it is not a general deduplication advantage. The equally informed reference matches the package. Polling adds reads over one final read without changing final reports or physical outcomes in Study A. Each readback policy still has 256 worlds with overlapping duplicate and wrong-payload events; detection does not repair those effects. These are finite-suite findings from 24 cases, not 15,360 independent tasks.

Study B was registered separately after Study A exposed the recoverable-lag coverage gap. Its [protocol](../output/recovery-availability-protocol-2026-10-20/protocol.json) has SHA-256 `e874c6781aa0dbc8ef682ea0a8d37c25d73cc861dada05fd0379116d4a7f6584`, protocol commit `b937920469285cdd971783fa6cb33216a7e4da13`, and source `d805b3e3560c05ee0a7efd02a80fc18503c6ee05`. The [raw summary](../output/recovery-availability-study-2026-10-20/summary.json) and normal [terminal receipt](../output/recovery-availability-study-2026-10-20/completion-receipt.json) retain all 960 completed worlds and zero failures: 192 per method. The [audit](../output/recovery-availability-audit-2026-10-20/audit-receipt.json) passed, verifying 1,925 artifacts and querying all 960 SQLite snapshots, including recorded-host byte checks. These checks are not original-runtime attestation or external replication.

The [full-grid coverage analysis](../output/recovery-availability-audit-2026-10-20/coverage-analysis.json) shows 192/192 safe recoveries per policy, one own effect per world, zero duplicate/unwanted events, and identical physical histories in all 192 five-arm cells. One final read yields 3/192 correct state/linkage reports and 189 unknown, using 192 reads with mean logical time 150 ms. Polling and reference yield 81/192 correct state/linkage reports and 111 unknown, using 741 reads with mean 224.375 ms. State-only has the same state yield, resources, and actions but abstains on all 192 linked reports. Response retry reports 192/192 correct state/linkage outcomes from ACK with zero reads and mean 2 ms, before horizon-qualified evidence. The uniform-success design cannot estimate its false-success risk.

The paired polling state-yield increase is 78/192 = 26/64 profiles = 0.40625, shared by the classical reference and state-only rule. Reference-polling reports, dispatches, and all metrics agree. Observed one-final-read versus polling coverage matches the prospective 1/64 versus 27/64 diagnostic; this agreement was not an audit pass condition. There is no primary physical-recovery gain. These controlled profiles repeat across three domains, rather than sampling independent tasks; simulated logical times are not production latency. No population confidence interval, p-value, framework-superiority result, or autonomous benchmark score follows.

## Study A collection-integrity deviation

When work resumed after a session interruption, the original terminal manifest and completion receipt were absent. The trial files, database snapshots, complete journal, and summary were retained. The original exit status and exact termination cause are unknown; filesystem modification times do not establish them.

A separately recorded [postcollection artifact finalization](../output/recovery-artifact-finalization-2026-10-20/finalization-record.json) preserves every existing artifact byte and creates only the missing packaging files. It does not resume or rerun worlds, replace failures, or regenerate the summary. It records a new source/runtime/claim recheck, the original inventory, and its own source and invocation. This is an explicit packaging deviation from the prospective no-resume protocol, not an uninterrupted or fully protocol-conforming runner completion.

The unchanged frozen [original-host semantic auditor](../output/recovery-controller-audit-2026-10-20/audit-receipt.json) and the [portable artifact audit](../output/recovery-portable-audit-2026-10-20/portable-audit-receipt.json) both passed, querying all 15,360 SQLite snapshots and recomputing the summary. They verify retained byte bindings, schedule, database lifecycles, and journal metrics. This independent code check is not external replication: its author also authored controllers, and its test author also authored the world. Hashes begin at the retained inventory and cannot prove absence of earlier tampering, off-record execution, or external activity. Study B produced its own terminal manifest and receipt normally; Study A's packaging deviation is not silently generalized to it or erased.

## Scoped paper, future extensions, and submission preparation

The completed objective experiments can form the empirical basis of a scoped software/systems paper about the declared protocol, conditional recovery behavior, reporting coverage, and read costs. They do not require a participant study when subjective claims are excluded. Broader external-validity claims would require extensions beyond this paper's current evidence:

- Evaluate a frozen autonomous agent in a maintained external environment, preserving native reward, matched classical recovery controls, and independently checked side effects.
- Validate adapter predicates, source authority, and provider finality/idempotency semantics against independently operated services or independently established ground truth. More internal SQLite seeds do not fill this gap.
- Preserve an immutable reviewer-accessible archive; select the actual venue and satisfy its length, anonymity, ethics, and AI-disclosure rules. The author is listed as an independent researcher; contact and venue-specific submission details remain outstanding.

The revisions make the question, comparisons, assumptions, and limits more reviewable. They do not by themselves establish external validity, methodological novelty, or acceptance at a top conference.
