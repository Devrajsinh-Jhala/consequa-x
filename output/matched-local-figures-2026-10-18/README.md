# Matched-study figures

These are descriptive figures from one explicitly closed collection. They do not establish causal attribution, model superiority, population generalization, or conference readiness.

Collection disposition: **completed**; planned 504; dispositions `{"completed": 503, "failed": 1, "interrupted_partial": 0, "unstarted": 0}`.

`matched-baseline-metrics` uses completed episodes with assessable ledger gold. Coverage is definite/assessable; error is wrong/definite; yield is correct/assessable. Zero denominators are null and displayed N/A. Abstaining on every target gives zero coverage and yield, with undefined claim error. Different auditor information budgets are declared in the protocol.

`matched-model-disposition` retains all planned slots in operational yield. Its action-selection and dispatch panel, when present, is a postcollection descriptive addition and uses completed episodes for its displayed denominator. A no-action episode does not exercise a dispatched fault. Full model tags and all fraction numerators/denominators are in `figure-fractions.csv` and `.json`.

SVG/PDF are vector exports; PNGs are layout previews. Exact input copies and their hashes are retained. Final inventory binds every generated file. No live model or analyzer was run.
