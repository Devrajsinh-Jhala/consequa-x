## What the evidence establishes

The implemented protocol and its declared-game behavior are supported in the
reported settings. Existing contrastive and Shapley estimators are adaptations;
the paper makes no claim of a new estimator or demonstrated superiority over an
equally informed baseline.

Retained evidence includes 1,280 original calibration trials, a separately frozen
384-trial shared-draw comparison, scripted refund enumeration, 2,048 repeated
scripted-simulator sampling trials and a 24-episode exploratory local-model
validation. The repeated sampler retained all 655,360 branches and 628,535
scripted calls, with zero model calls. Availability, reference eligibility,
coverage, width and directional flags remain separate quantities.

The local-model validation used one Qwen3 4B checkpoint and 59 calls. Six
contradictions among ten assessable original-profile claims have a descriptive
95% Wilson interval of approximately [0.313, 0.832]. That interval does not
establish calibrated model-population uncertainty over shared cases. Zero
definite revised-profile statuses leaves its contradiction rate undefined.
Rationale annotations were unblinded and AI assisted; independent human
verification and inter-rater reliability remain unestablished.

Six previously retained [vector figures](figures/README.md) depict evidence flow, a timeout/retry
sequence, the full branch inventory, coverage/precision/detection tradeoffs,
matched factual-auditor results and model/profile execution accounting.
The working revision adds a [same-evidence judge plot](figures/same-evidence-trace-judge.svg)
and [benchmark state-transfer plot](figures/benchmark-state-transfer.svg),
plus [Figure 9: native CAR interval presentation](figures/upstream-car-intervals.svg).
Plotting reuses audited results and adds no simulation or model calls; a new
compiled revision still requires its own source/figure/PDF quality receipt.

The matched study adds three small local model families and fourteen fixed
case/wording cells, not 504 independently sampled tasks. Consequa-X and the
same-information reference agree on all 503 completed prediction dictionaries.
State and linked correct yields are 431/503 and 385/503; full-planned yields
retain denominator 504. Gemma dispatched 0/168 actions, so its correct negative
state observations do not establish refund execution or fault handling.
The [separate AI-assisted audit](../output/matched-local-audit-2026-10-18/README.md)
checks source bindings, inventories, the journal chain and aggregate fractions;
it is not independent human validation.

All 900 action captures exercised the public verification/explanation boundary;
all 900 withheld-readback checks stayed unknown, origin stayed unknown and
assessment alignment stayed unassessable. This is model-free task/tool state
transfer, not full task reward, autonomous benchmark performance, pass@k or

separate from execution, and supplies no independent human labels.

## Current software and historical experiments

Current software is [2.1.1](https://pypi.org/project/consequa-x/2.1.1/), with
[retained release records](../output/release-2.1.1-2026-10-19/README.md) and a
[source/publication review](../output/claude-2.1.1-paper-review-2026-10-19/README.md).
The 2.1 additions include composite verifiers, several readback sources,
OpenTelemetry export/capture and optional SDK integrations. Version 2.1.1 keeps
the 2.0 API and schema 1.0. Its release and integration engineering checks are
separate from scientific validation; these additions have no new scientific
feature evaluation in this paper.

The source-bound review checks all 54 wheel runtime payloads against build
`b73ecd00fc9b4b8b3f9b8182aa8ed652419d7e54`. Retained publication receipts
report upload at 15:15 UTC on 19 October. A subsequent
[read-only official registry check](../output/claude-2.1.1-paper-review-2026-10-19/registry-verification.json)
also matches both retained distribution sizes and hashes. Compared with measured 2.0,
36 runtime paths are unchanged, 12 modified and six added, with none deleted.
The display name is now ConseqaX; package/CLI `consequa-x`, import
`consequa_x` and historical study filenames remain unchanged.

Retained 2.1.1 engineering evidence includes three actual-model SDK smoke loops:
local Ollama through OpenAI Agents and LangChain, and authenticated remote Haiku
through Claude Agent SDK. These are integration checks, not the frozen research
studies or new accuracy measurements. The Claude loop used an in-process SDK MCP
server; no external/deployed MCP service or live OpenTelemetry collector/backend
validation is established. The default refund verifier class body, records,
explanation, target helper and numerical modules match frozen 2.0, while the
public `verify`/`averify` dispatch wrappers changed for multiple readbacks.
Those source similarities do not extend the measured conformance to all 2.1.1.

The historical [2.0.0 release record](../output/release-2.0.0-2026-10-19/README.md) binds the

The [2.0 source/API review](../output/claude-2.0-paper-review-2026-10-19/README.md)

a declared time base, keyword-only wire records and deeply read-only payloads.
Responses, assessments and receipts identify their action. Verifiers now own
target matching and findings; `explain` returns structured per-action results
from the latest recorded receipt and assessment. Legacy matcher interfaces are
removed, and research imports move to `consequa_x.research`.

The matched model, local-judge, native tool-transfer and native CAR collections
retain their frozen 1.1.0 sources and schema 0.1 artifacts. Earlier studies
retain their own original versions and sources. The breaking 2.0 release is not an
end-to-end equivalent artifact established by those experiments. Its new
multi-action, polling, retry-advice, async, collection and presentation APIs
were not evaluated by the frozen studies.

## Research still open

A stronger comparative paper still needs intervention/controller and
structural-attribution comparisons,
bounded watermark polling with availability versus wait time, a safe real-API
test-mode study, and autonomous benchmark evaluation with its required user,
communication and qualitative grading components. The local judge, model-free
tau3 transfer and native CAR compatibility study address narrower questions;
they do not complete those experiments. Held-out fault/task populations,
independent blinded human annotation, real-service cost, modern bounded-mean
intervals, multiplicity control and more precise coverage studies remain
separate requirements.

The repository is now public. A complete immutable archive, evidence-access
inventory, author contact metadata, final independent human checking and
venue requirements remain submission steps. The supplied affiliation is
Independent Researcher; no institution, archive DOI, human validation,
acceptance or actual submission is invented. GitHub Actions remains disabled.
