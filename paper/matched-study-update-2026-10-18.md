## Analysis and safeguards

The five factual auditors are response-only, final structured self-report,
readback without provenance, an independent same-information contract/provenance
reference, and Consequa-X state/origin verification. Gold comes from a separate
simulator ledger predicate. They are information ablations and a transparent
reference, not faithful CAR, LLM-judge or other causal-attribution reproductions.
No baseline makes extra model calls. Parity with the equally informed reference
is an informative correctness check, not a superiority result.

An independent AI-assisted code/data audit checked the first six completed
episodes and their request/response bindings. All six were genuine no-dispatch
decisions. Consequently, additional execution diagnostics were specified
**after collection began**, before final analysis: action-selection, actual
dispatch, retry, effect counts and realized fault exposure. They prevent
empty-ledger correctness from being described as refund execution or fault
handling. These diagnostics are descriptive additions; the frozen models,
prompts, schedule, primary metrics and stopping rules remain unchanged.
The audit records are under `output/study-audit-2026-10-18/`; AI-assisted checking
does not count as independent human validation.

Final analysis must wait for a closed, valid journal with no active runner lock.
It will retain completed, failed, interrupted and unstarted counts globally and
within every planned stratum, including empty groups. Definite coverage, error
among definite claims, correct-definite yield and full-planned operational yield
remain separate. No denominator of zero becomes a perfect score. No pooled
independent-task confidence interval or significance claim is planned.
